# Mimic

Memory across AI conversations.

Mimic gives Claude a private workspace for remembering and finding notes across
conversations. Save titled memories, add text or Markdown files, ask questions or use
keywords, and request a short preview when you need orientation. Source details and
downloads are available separately. Product feedback goes privately to the service
operators and is kept outside your memory.

## Connect

1. Add the plugin, then connect Mimic (claude.ai: the plugin's Connectors tab or
   Settings → Connectors; Claude Code: `/mcp` → mimic → Authenticate).
2. A browser page asks for your email. Click **Send code**, then enter the one-time code.
3. The operator activates your workspace. Until then only Identity and Handbook are
   available. New accounts begin with an empty workspace.

Use a computer for the first sign-in. Email verification and workspace activation
are separate steps; the operator manages activation.

## Tools

Find, Remember, Upload, Metadata, Identity, Handbook, Activity and Feedback. The included
"Using Mimic" skill explains how Claude should use them. Retrieved notes can inform
decisions, but do not grant permission to act.
